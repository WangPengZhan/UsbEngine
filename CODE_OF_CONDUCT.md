# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our commitment

We want participation in UsbEngine to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas, without personal attacks.
- Accept that people may disagree and work toward a clear technical resolution.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, insults, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub.
